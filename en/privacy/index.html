1. Scope and Roles

This policy applies to the public pages hosted at NohGangWorks.github.io. It does not replace the privacy policy of a NohGang Works product, an app store, an operating system provider, an email provider, or a third-party site linked from this website.

NohGang Works Co.,Ltd. is the controller of inquiry information it receives. GitHub, Inc. operates GitHub Pages and processes hosting and security data under GitHub's own terms and privacy statement.

Providing information by email is voluntary. If information necessary to understand or answer an inquiry is not provided, we may be unable to respond.

3. Disclosure, Service Providers, and International Processing

We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, rent mailing lists, or disclose inquiry information to third parties for their own marketing.

Information may be disclosed when you direct us to do so, when necessary to protect rights or safety, or when lawfully required by a court or public authority. Cloudflare, Inc. routes email sent to the company domain and may process message addresses, headers, content, attachments, routing events, and security data to deliver and protect email. GitHub may process hosting data in the United States and other locations described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Cloudflare's processing is described in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

4. Cookies, Tracking, and Do Not Track

NohGang Works does not place first-party analytics or advertising cookies and does not track visitors across unaffiliated websites. Because we do not perform such tracking, browser Do Not Track signals and Global Privacy Control signals do not change our practices. The hosting provider may use essential technologies under its own policy.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to know or access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or receive information about disclosures. Send a request to the privacy contact above and identify the email address or inquiry involved. We may verify identity and may retain the minimum record needed to document the request.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right. We do not use automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

6. United States Privacy Disclosures

For California Online Privacy Protection Act purposes, the categories described above are the categories collected through inquiry communications; the recipients are service providers and authorities only as described in Section 3. Consumers may request review or correction using the process in Section 5. We notify users of material policy changes by changing this page, its effective date, and, when appropriate, a prominent website notice.

NohGang Works does not sell or share personal information and therefore does not provide a “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” link. If a U.S. state privacy law applies to a particular request, we will provide the rights and response required by that law.

7. Children

This general-audience corporate website is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that a child under 13 has submitted personal information without required parental authorization, we will delete it. The website likewise does not knowingly process personal information of a child under 14 in Korea without the consent required by Korean law.

8. Security and Deletion

We minimize collection, restrict access to inquiry communications, use access-controlled email and hosting services, and delete information according to the periods above. Electronic records scheduled for deletion are removed so they are not reasonably recoverable in ordinary operations.
