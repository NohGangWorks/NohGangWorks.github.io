2. Service Providers and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. We disclose information only at your direction, to comply with law, or to protect rights and safety.

GitHub processes website hosting and security data, and Cloudflare routes company email. They may process data in the United States and other locations under the GitHub Privacy Statement and Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

3. Deletion, Tracking, and Your Rights

We delete information when its retention period ends. You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or another right available where you live by using the contact below.

We do not use first-party analytics, advertising tracking, or significant automated decisions. Because we do not track visitors across unrelated sites, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals do not change our practices.

4. Children and Security

This general-audience site is not directed to children under 13 or, in Korea, children under 14. If we learn that a child's information was submitted without required authorization, we delete it.

We minimize collection, restrict access to inquiries, protect service accounts, and manage retention periods.
