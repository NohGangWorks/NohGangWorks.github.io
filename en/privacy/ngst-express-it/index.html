NGST Express It Privacy Policy

This policy explains how NohGang Works Co.,Ltd. handles information in connection with NGST Express It.

NGST Express It processes content on your device. If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app sends settings and user-created templates directly to your personal Drive for synchronization. The company does not receive these files through a company-operated synchronization server. Feature availability depends on your platform and app version.

1. On-Device Information

The app processes settings, templates, work files and user-selected content on the device. Session data stays local and is excluded from cloud synchronization. Local usage counts and interface state are not sent to a company analytics service. You control exports, sharing destinations and any device-to-device features you use.

2. Optional Google Drive Synchronization

Your choice and purpose. In Normal or Expert mode, you can connect a Google account through Google’s official authorization interface to keep app settings and user-created derived templates available across your devices. The app does not receive your Google password or require a NohGang Works account. Easy mode does not run cloud synchronization.

Data accessed and transferred. The app uploads and downloads Program Options files and user-created template files, including text, settings, file references and other data contained in those files. Templates may contain personal information you enter. It also processes file identifiers, names, edit times, hashes and deletion markers to detect changes, and account storage capacity to display app storage usage. Session data is not included, and the app does not upload all files on your device or request access to your entire Drive.

Permissions and location. The drive.file permission allows access to files created by or made available to this app. New cloud data is stored in the app-created NGST/expressit folder. The drive.appdata permission is used to read and migrate older hidden app-data backups; new files use the visible folder. Old hidden copies may remain after migration. Google processes and hosts the cloud data under your account and its Privacy Policy, potentially on servers outside your country.

Retention and protection. Settings and templates are stored in readable INI/JSON formats; the app does not add end-to-end encryption to those cloud files. Connections to Google use HTTPS. Access tokens and any refresh tokens are kept separately in platform-protected device storage, not in synchronized settings or templates. The app also keeps local synchronization records and pending downloads to reconcile updates. These can contain copies of synchronized files. Last-sync time, transferred bytes and storage usage are displayed in the app, not sent to a company analytics server.

Use and sharing. Google user data is used only to provide the synchronization and storage information you request. It is not sold, used for advertising, credit decisions, or training general-purpose AI models. Company staff do not routinely read your cloud files. Content you voluntarily send with a support request is handled as inquiry information. If you separately connect another supported provider, synchronized app files may also be uploaded to that selected account. A provider that is merely listed but not connected does not receive these files.

NGST Express It’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. New providers or materially different data uses will be described before they are enabled, with consent where required.

3. External Services and Email

The app does not upload work content, identifiers, or usage analytics to a company server. App stores, operating-system features, external apps, and sharing destinations apply their own policies when selected by the user.

If you email us, we use the address, supplied name, message, and attachments to respond. Ordinary inquiries are deleted within 30 days after the final response; consumer complaint or dispute records may be kept for 3 years when required by Korean e-commerce law. See the Website Privacy Policy for Cloudflare email routing.

4. Disclosure, Tracking, and Automated Decisions

We do not sell app personal information, share it for targeted advertising, use analytics or tracking SDKs, or make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals do not change app behavior because the app does not perform behavioral tracking.

5. Deletion and Your Rights

You can disconnect a provider in the app and revoke its access in your Google Account connections. Disconnecting removes that device’s saved connection credentials; it does not delete local content, existing cloud files or credentials on another device. Changing app mode also does not delete cloud data. Local synchronization records and pending downloads may remain until app data is removed.

To delete cloud copies, disconnect all devices, delete the app-created NGST/expressit files and empty the provider’s trash where applicable. If you previously used hidden app-data backups, remove those through Google Drive’s app-management controls as well. A connected device or a retained legacy backup can otherwise restore data. Provider backup retention and deletion follow the provider’s policy. To remove local copies, use the app or operating system’s file/app-data controls, including local cloud caches; retain an export first if you need one.

We delete inquiry information when its retention period ends. You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or other rights available where you live through the contact below. The company cannot directly delete files held only in your device or personal cloud account; those are managed using the controls described above.

6. Children and Security

The app is not directed to children under 13 or, in Korea, children under 14. If we learn that a child's information was submitted without required authorization, we delete it.

Cloud storage is optional and uses the protections described above. Data formats are readable so you can manage your files; choose appropriate device and cloud-account access controls. We update this policy and provide any required notice or consent request before introducing materially different processing.
