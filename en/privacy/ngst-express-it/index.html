1. On-Device Information

The app processes settings, templates, work files, text, images, video references, and other user-selected content locally on the device to provide editing, display, export, and related functions. NohGang Works does not receive this content through app functionality.

The user controls local files through the operating system. Uninstalling the app may remove app-specific settings, but files saved outside the app's private storage may remain until the user deletes them.

2. Network Features and Third Parties

The current app does not upload work content, identifiers, diagnostics, or usage analytics to a NohGang Works server. If the user opens a website, app store, operating-system feature, external application, cloud folder, or sharing destination, that third party may process information under its own policy.

App stores and distributors may independently process purchase, licensing, device, update, and diagnostic information. NohGang Works receives only information those platforms make available under their terms and does not control their independent collection.

3. Support Inquiries

If you contact us, we process your email address, sender name if supplied, message, attachments, and correspondence to answer the inquiry. Cloudflare, Inc. routes inbound mail sent to the company domain under its privacy policy. Ordinary inquiries are deleted within 30 days after the final response. Records reasonably needed for a paid-product complaint, dispute, fraud prevention, or legal claim may be retained for up to 3 years, or longer only when required by law.

4. Sale, Sharing, Tracking, and Automated Decisions

NohGang Works does not sell or share app personal information, use it for targeted advertising, track users across apps or websites, or make automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects. Because the app does not perform behavioral tracking, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals do not change app behavior.

5. Rights and Requests

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other rights available under applicable law for information NohGang Works holds, such as support correspondence. Send requests to the contact below. We may verify identity and will not discriminate for exercising a privacy right. Local app content is controlled directly by the user on the device and is not accessible to NohGang Works.

6. United States and California Disclosures

The app itself collects no categories of personally identifiable information. Support-email categories, purposes, recipients, retention, review and correction process, policy-change process, tracking response, and third-party practices are described above. NohGang Works does not sell or share personal information. If a U.S. state privacy law applies to a request, we will honor the rights required by that law.

7. Children

NGST Express It is a general-audience productivity application and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a child sends personal information to support without required parental authorization, we will delete it after learning of the submission.

8. Security and Future Features

The app's local-first design minimizes transfer of personal information. Users should protect their devices and exported files. If a future version introduces accounts, network synchronization, telemetry, advertising, or another collection feature, this policy and any required in-app notice or consent will be updated before or when the feature is introduced.
