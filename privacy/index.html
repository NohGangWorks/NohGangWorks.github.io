1. Purposes, Items, and Retention Periods

This website does not provide account registration, login, comments, contact forms, payment forms, advertising tracking, or first-party analytics scripts. NohGang Works does not directly collect personal information through website functionality.

If you contact NohGang Works by email, your email address and the information included in your message may be processed only to respond to and manage the inquiry. Such information is retained only for the period necessary to handle the inquiry and related dispute response, then deleted without undue delay unless retention is required by applicable law.

2. Third-Party Disclosure

NohGang Works does not disclose personal information collected through this website to third parties.

3. Entrustment of Personal Information Processing

NohGang Works does not entrust separate personal information processing for this website. The website is served as static files through GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process technical information necessary to operate and secure its hosting service under its own policies.

4. Destruction of Personal Information

Because this website does not directly collect personal information through website functionality, there is no separate website-collected personal information to destroy. Inquiry emails are deleted without undue delay after the purpose of processing is achieved, unless retention is required by applicable law.

5. Data Subject Rights and How to Exercise Them

You may request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing of your personal information. Requests can be sent to the privacy contact listed below and will be handled under applicable law after identity verification where necessary.

6. Cookies and Automatic Collection Technologies

NohGang Works does not operate first-party cookies, advertising identifiers, web analytics scripts, or similar technologies on this website to identify or track visitors.

7. Security Measures

NohGang Works keeps personal information processing to a minimum and limits access to inquiry emails to the purpose of responding to those inquiries. If features that collect personal information are added later, NohGang Works will apply appropriate safeguards such as access control, retention management, and encryption where necessary.
