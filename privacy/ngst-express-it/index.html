1. Purposes, Items, and Retention Periods

NGST Express It does not collect personal information through account registration, login, advertising, analytics SDKs, remote server transmission, or cloud synchronization. Data created or edited in the app is processed on the user's device.

If you contact NohGang Works by email, your email address and the information included in your message may be processed only to respond to and manage the inquiry. Such information is retained only for the period necessary to handle the inquiry and related dispute response, then deleted without undue delay unless retention is required by applicable law.

2. On-Device Data Processing

App settings, work files, and user-created content are processed on the user's device. NohGang Works does not transmit that data to a server or collect it in a separate storage system. If the user exports files or shares them with another app, cloud service, or messaging service, the external service's own policy may apply.

3. Third-Party Disclosure

NohGang Works does not disclose personal information collected through NGST Express It to third parties.

4. Entrustment of Personal Information Processing

NohGang Works does not entrust separate personal information processing for NGST Express It. App stores, operating systems, payment providers, or distribution platforms may process information under their own policies.

5. Destruction of Personal Information

Because NGST Express It does not directly collect personal information through app functionality, there is no separate app-collected personal information to destroy. Inquiry emails are deleted without undue delay after the purpose of processing is achieved, unless retention is required by applicable law.

6. Data Subject Rights and How to Exercise Them

You may request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing of your personal information. Requests can be sent to the privacy contact listed below and will be handled under applicable law after identity verification where necessary.

7. Automatic Collection and Tracking Technologies

NGST Express It does not use advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs, or first-party cookies. If these features are introduced later, NohGang Works will disclose the collected items and purposes in advance and obtain consent where required.

8. Security Measures

NohGang Works designs NGST Express It so that app functionality does not collect personal information. If future features require personal information processing, NohGang Works will apply appropriate safeguards such as data minimization, access limitation, retention management, and other necessary protective measures.
